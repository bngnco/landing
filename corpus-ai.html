Corpus AI · In development at Verifyco

We are building a big eye.

Corpus AI is a detection model with a visual memory. It studies how cameras, editors, generators and platforms leave their marks, keeps that memory in a curated corpus, and reads every new file against it. Then it explains: what the engine saw, what it could not check, and what to look at next.

01 · What it is

One model, three organs.

A detector score is a number. Corpus AI is a system: something that looks, something that remembers, and something that decides what the first two are allowed to conclude.

The eye

Looks at the file at the resolution it was made.

A detection model that reads native-resolution views of an image and sampled frames of a video, not a downscaled thumbnail. It runs on the phone where it can and on the server where it must, under one contract, so both give the same verdict for the same evidence.

Model · training runs in progress
The memory

Remembers how marks are made.

A curated corpus where every file carries its source, its transformation chain and its hash: which generator made it, which editor touched it, which chat app or platform re-encoded it. Locked holdouts are never used for training, so a benchmark number can be trusted when it is published.

Corpus · growing, licensed, grouped
The judgment

Decides what each signal may conclude.

A written rule set says what every signal is allowed to claim. Corpus AI reads the engine's own layers, cites them, keeps counterevidence in view and answers "inconclusive" when the evidence is thin, instead of inventing confidence.

Rule set · live in the Verifyco engine today

    03 · Why a memory

    A sentence is not evidence.

    In 2026, confident one-line verdicts started doing damage. BOOM's report on its August to October fact-checks records chatbots telling users that real protest photos were not genuine, and a news channel running a fact-check video on that basis before deleting it.

    What we believe

    Detectors will lose the race against generators, and that is fine.

    Every new generator weakens yesterday's detector, and a platform re-encode erases most of what a detector looks for. The lasting answer is a memory that updates, signed provenance where it exists, and a tool that is honest about everything in between.

    06 · The next twelve months

    The memory first, then the eye, then the voice.

    Planned work, in order. Nothing here is a promise of a date; everything here is already being built.

    1. NowThe 2026 video corpus

      Generator video as it actually looks after X, TikTok and YouTube re-encode it, with provenance and transformation chains recorded for every file. A locked benchmark that is never used for training.

    2. NextNative-resolution retrain

      Two models under one contract: a mobile base model for the phone and a larger web specialist for faces and video. Trained on native-resolution views, evaluated on the locked benchmark.

    3. ThenMemory retrieval and the console

      A new file compared against generator families and re-encode signatures in the corpus. The console opens as a private preview to design partners, with the conversation grounded in the engine's cited layers.

    4. GatePublished model card

      Measured calibration, generalisation and failure modes, published before the preview is called a product. No number appears on this page before it is measured.

    07 · Living model card

    Trust requires published boundaries.

    No invented benchmark score and no vague promise. These are the gates Verifyco intends to document before a Corpus AI preview is treated as a product.

    Defined

    Scope

    Read a file against the visual memory; reason over the engine's layer outputs; cite supporting evidence, counterevidence and unknowns; recommend the next check.

    Not autonomous adjudication · a human decides
    Evaluation target

    Protocol and calibration

    Held-out sources, manipulation families and compression conditions that never touch training; citation correctness and calibrated uncertainty measured on the locked benchmark.

    Metrics published after measurement
    Evaluation target

    Known failure modes

    Heavy recompression, missing originals, haze and motion blur, domain shift and conflicts between layers can all produce inconclusive results. They are recorded, not hidden.

    "Inconclusive" remains a valid outcome
    Open boundary

    Privacy and deployment

    Device and server split, retention, training-data use and deletion controls are release-gated decisions, published before preview access.

    Local files stay local wherever the eye can run on the device
