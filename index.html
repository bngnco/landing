03 · HOW IT WORKS

Five signals. One verdict.

Content credentials, metadata forensics, neural analysis, temporal consistency and frequency diagnostics — combined under one evidence contract.

01

Originality stamp

C2PA content credentials verify signed provenance and declared edit history when a trusted manifest is present.

02

File fingerprint

Available EXIF, encoder and container fields are checked for provenance cues and internal inconsistencies.

03

Face review

An on-device neural model screens sampled image regions and video frames for learned synthetic-media artifacts.

04

Motion check

Frame-to-frame sampling reports temporal instability for video and animated media as diagnostic context.

05

Pattern check

Spectral analysis records compression and frequency regularities without allowing an uncalibrated threshold to control the verdict.
